Our company

A story
born in Thiès.

An industrial story born in Thiès and developed alongside field professionals.

Founded in 1978, Entreprise de Construction Métallique et Mécanique supports farmers, craftspeople, cooperatives, NGOs and institutions with accessible, durable mechanical solutions designed for local needs.

Our mission

To design, manufacture and supply durable mechanical solutions that strengthen productivity, product quality and the economic autonomy of farmers and processors.
